STORY

A LEGACY WRITTEN IN BLOOD

Centuries ago, Muzan Kibutsuji became the first demon, marking the beginning of a dark era in which humanity lived under the constant threat of creatures that hunted after sunset. As the number of demons grew, the Demon Slayer Corps emerged to protect the innocent, passing down the art of swordsmanship and developing powerful Breathing Styles to combat their enemies. During Japan's Sengoku period, Yoriichi Tsugikuni, the greatest demon slayer in history, developed Sun Breathing, the original technique from which other Breathing Styles originated. Despite coming close to defeating Muzan, the battle against demons continued for generations, with countless swordsmen sacrificing their lives in hopes of ending his reign.

In the Taisho era, a young boy named Tanjiro Kamado returned home to discover that his family had been slaughtered, with his younger sister, Nezuko, being the only survivor—though she had been transformed into a demon. Determined to restore her humanity and avenge his family, Tanjiro joined the Demon Slayer Corps and began a dangerous journey filled with relentless training, powerful enemies, and unexpected friendships. Alongside Zenitsu Agatsuma, Inosuke Hashibira, and the Hashira, he uncovered secrets surrounding Muzan's origins and the long history of demon slaying. As the conflict intensified, the Corps faced its greatest battle yet, bringing together the strength, sacrifices, and determination of generations in a final struggle that would decide the fate of humanity and determine whether the world could finally be free from Muzan's darkness.
